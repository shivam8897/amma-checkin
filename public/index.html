Your father won't open a health app. But he has answered the phone every day of his life.

Any phone works

A landline, a basic keypad phone or a smartphone. They answer and press a number, that's all.

Their own language

Every call speaks to them the way family does, by the name they like, in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, Malayalam, Bangla or English.

You stay close

No more wondering at your desk in Bengaluru or Dubai. If something is off, your phone tells you first.

How one call goes

About 60 seconds, at the time you choose. If they miss it, we try again after 15 minutes.

  1. A familiar greeting

    We greet them by name and say you asked us to call. The first call asks for their permission, and they can stop calls anytime by pressing 0.

  2. One question per medicine

    "Have you taken your BP tablet?" Press 1 for yes, 2 for no. If they haven't, we gently remind them and tell you.

  3. How are you feeling?

    Good, okay, or not well. "Not well" sends you an alert immediately.

  4. Sugar reading, if you want it

    They type the number from their glucometer. Readings that are too high or too low reach you right away.

  5. A message from you

    Type a few words in your dashboard, like "Amma, I'll video call on Sunday", and we read it out in their language on the next call.

  6. Press 9, anytime

    If they want to talk to you, they press 9. You and your emergency contact get an urgent WhatsApp at once.

Silence when all is well. A message when it isn't.

You only hear from us when something needs you. Urgent alerts also go to a neighbour or relative nearby, so someone can knock on the door.

Questions families ask

Will my parents find it confusing?

They only answer and press one number. Every question says which number to press, and if they don't press anything we ask once more, then move on.

Does it speak like a robot?

The voice speaks naturally in their language and calls them by the name you choose, like "Lakshmi garu" or "Amma". Even better: record the questions in your own voice from the dashboard, and Amma hears you asking about her tablets every morning.

What if they don't pick up?

We wait 15 minutes and call again. If they still don't answer, you and your emergency contact get a WhatsApp alert.

Is this a medical or emergency service?

No. Amma Check-in is a daily reminder and check-in. In an emergency, call 112 in India. Always follow your parents' doctor's advice.

Who can see the information?

Only the family members you add. We never sell data, and you can delete everything from your dashboard at any time.

Can my parents stop the calls?

Yes. They can press 0 on the first call, and you can pause or stop calls from your dashboard whenever you like.
